## Judgment merge

- `styped` is no longer an inductive. It is the definition
  `styped Φ Ψ Δ F t T := typed wnil Φ Ψ ∅ Δ F t T`, the source instance
  of the one judgment `typed`.
- Γ stays the Rocq triple (W, Ψ, Φ): world, box hypotheses, future
  hypotheses. The paper writes it as one Γ.
- The new `typed_world_transfer` moves a Θ = ∅ derivation from a
  well-formed world of empty support to any world. It replaces the two
  former inductions, `styped_typed` and `typed_styped`, which keep their
  statements.
- `soundness` and `soundness_source` keep their statements.
- This is the cheap faithful version, recorded in SHARP-EDGES entry 153.
  Fusing W, Ψ and Φ into one constraint would rework PointSubst.v and
  Fundamental.v.

## Audit summary (`docs/PAPER-AUDIT.md`)

About 120 claims were checked. 39 mismatches were found and fixed in the
paper. No Rocq file changed. The main fixes:

- **Rule premises:** E-Const asks the instance to be known only for
  force. E-Pack gains "p⃗ known". E-Open gains "T not a □ type". Q-Recv
  gains `recv_ok`. Q-Send gains a disjointness premise. P-Connect uses a
  separate Σ′_C dual at aliased points. A missing R-Choice rule was added
  to the reduction semantics.
- **Definitions:**
  - Creation-constraint acyclicity is relative to Γ.
  - Strictness includes well-formedness.
  - The quantifier direction of world extension in the logical
    relation was reversed (W′ ⊑ W became W′ ⊒ W).
  - A future has at most one future variable.
  - wf has three conditions.
- **Statements:**
  - The compositionality lemmas for E-Abs and E-Exp now carry their
    real hypotheses.
  - Connect cites `conn_all` and `Prel_connect`.
  - The PGV theorem is stated at unit type with o < pr(T) at send and
    receive.
  - PN states its fragment limits.
  - The subsumption intro is limited to mechanized fragments.
  - The box rejection now claims only ¬(ι ≺ ι′).
  - The `slt_acyclic_nil` scope is stated.
  - The Kobayashi forwarder uses a lexicographic order, not a ℤ rank.
- **Examples:** the "exactly one reduction sequence" claims for sync and
  cycle were false and are gone. The blocked and badlink deadlocks are
  marked as not mechanized.
- **Delegation gap:** the carrier is send then close, at priorities 0 and
  10. A finite delegated session of at most nine actions fits the gap,
  and an unbounded one fits no gap.
- **Counts:** lemmas and theorems are 3,541, not 3,519. The earlier
  count left out the `Local Lemma`s.
- Choice indices are made consistent: select₀ and select₁, ⊕{S₀,S₁},
  inj_{i+1}.

Three minor mismatches were left on purpose and are listed in the audit:
- `proc` does not require closed terms.
- `pscoped` admits constants in payloads.
- V-Const exists only at arrows.

The audit file also lists every claim the paper makes about other papers,
with its cite key, for a local check.

## Not done, and why

- **The blocked and badlink deadlocks have no Rocq proof.** Proving them
  needs a stuck-state analysis of two threads closing different
  channels, which is not a trivial corollary. The paper now says the
  deadlocks are not mechanized.
- **Mordido–Pérez:** the paper relates it informally, as the author
  decided. Rocq still has `mp3_deadlock_free_source`, and
  5-formalization.tex mentions it as mechanized but treated informally.
- **The two `Stream.v` files clash in rocqrefs.** examples/Stream.v and
  examples/cfst/Stream.v share a base name, so rocqrefs cannot cite the
  cfst `st_deadlock_free`. The paper cites `client_typed` and
  `newclient_typed` instead.
- **The 151 baseline figure** could not be reproduced (see above).
