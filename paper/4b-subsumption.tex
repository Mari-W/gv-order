\section{Subsumption}
\label{sec:ex-priority}

A mechanized fragment of each of two priority-based systems translates
into \OurLang, and the translation makes every closed program of unit
type in the fragment deadlock free as written, not only its image.
Both systems give the two ends of a channel one priority or level, so their
images live in the collapsed instance of \cref{sec:app-endpoints}. The
world $\wrld_{\mathrm{PGV}}$ and the top-level constraint of the second
translation identify every point of the image with its partner point,
$\fEq{\pntA}{\pdual{\pntA}}$, and the gate then compares a blocking
point with the points of the held ends, as a priority check does.

\begin{theorem}[A fragment of PGV \rocq{pgv_deadlock_free_source}]
\label{thm:pgv}
  Every closed program of type $\tyUnit$ of the fragment of
  \citet{KokkeD23} cut out by \rocq{pgv_mono} in \rocq{pgv_typed} is deadlock free. The
  fragment is PGV without $\asTy{0}$ and $\asTmId{absurd}$, with the
  premise $o < \mathit{pr}(\ty)$ at send and receive, the premise
  $q < \mathit{pr}(\ty)$ at their pair rule, and monotone session types
  at every send and receive.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  Two halves. First, the translation types. One fixed world $\wrld_{\mathrm{PGV}}$
  ranks the points of the image lexicographically by the priority a
  session type carries, and it is strict (\rocq{Wpgv},
  \rocq{Wpgv_strict}). Induction on the PGV derivation types the
  translated term in that world (\rocq{pgv_translate}), where PGV's
  bound $p$ becomes a point below the future. \Cref{th:soundness} then makes the
  translation of a closed program deadlock free
  (\rocq{ptyped_closed}, \rocq{pgv_deadlock_free}).

  Second, the translation changes the term only at an unapplied
  communication constant, which it wraps in a $\lambda$
  (\rocq{pgv_eta}), so deadlock freedom carries back. A congruence
  relates a term to its translation and contains that wrapping
  (\rocq{rel}, \rocq{rel_eta}). Every reduction of $\asTm{M}$ is matched
  by a sequence of reductions of the translation
  (\rocq{PGVSimulation.sim_step}, \rocq{PGVSimulation.sim_star}). When no thread of $\asTm{M}$ takes a $\beta$,
  $\tNew$ or $\tSpawn$ step, $\beta$ steps bring every thread of the
  translation to the action of its thread in $\asTm{M}$ (\rocq{norm}),
  so a step of the translation is a step of $\asTm{M}$
  (\rocq{PGVSimulation.final}). A stuck $\asTm{M}$ would leave the translation stuck.
\end{proof}

The fragment leaves out the empty type $\asTy{0}$ of
\citet{KokkeD23} and its eliminator $\asTmId{absurd}$
(\rocq{pgv_typed}). \OurLang\ has no empty type, so neither form has an
image.

PGV's send schema puts no condition on its payload, and our fragment
adds $o < \mathit{pr}(\ty)$ for $\sSend{\ty}{\sty}$ and
$\sRecv{\ty}{\sty}$ at priority $o$. The
embedding is what found the gap. Without that condition a closed PGV
program types and deadlocks, and the \OurLang\ premise $\tSend$ owes,
$\fLt{\pntA}{\pnode}$ for every head $\pnode$ of the payload, is the
same requirement. Their pair rule compares the bound of the left
component with the environment of the right one and asks nothing of the
left component's type, which costs a second program, and the fragment
adds $q < \mathit{pr}(\ty)$ there. \ref{rule:E-Pair} types the right
component under $\frm$ grown by $\heads(\ty)$, which is that premise.
PGV with both premises rejects both programs for every choice of the
fragment's predicate (\rocq{cx1_rejected}, \rocq{cx2_rejected}).

The theorem covers monotone sessions. A falling session, whose
priorities decrease along a channel, types in repaired PGV
(\rocq{fall2_typed}, \rocq{fallxy_typed}). The program of
\rocq{fallxy_typed} defeats every translation that reads points off
session types alone, and its typing in \OurLang\ is not mechanized.

Example~2.1 of \citet{KokkeD23} is among the mechanized programs

\begin{theorem}[Padovani and Novara \rocq{pn_deadlock_free_source}]
\label{thm:pn}
  Every closed program of type unit of \citet{PadovaniN15}, typed in
  their Table~2 with T-App-Poly and the repaired $\asTmId{fix}$
  (\rocq{pn_typed}), is deadlock free in their semantics. In the
  fragment of \rocq{pn_typed} constants occur applied and sums range
  over unlimited types.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
  The same two halves. The translation of a closed program is typable in
  the source judgment under one top-level constraint over one point
  variable (\rocq{pn_translate_source}), that constraint is acyclic
  (\rocq{PNWorld.Crel}, \rocq{Crel_acyclic}), and
  \cref{thm:soundness-source} makes the translation deadlock free
  (\rocq{pn_deadlock_free}).

  Carrying that back is a simulation. A relation ties a source term to
  its target at the shape of the P\&N type the translation reads, it
  holds of every translation the typing produces, and it is closed under
  substitution (\rocq{trel}, \rocq{trel_of_typed},
  \rocq{trel_subst}). Every source step is matched by the corresponding
  target step together with administrative ones (\rocq{PNSimulation.fwd_rt}), and a
  related source term is a value or steps (\rocq{src_progress}), which
  turns deadlock freedom of the target into deadlock freedom of the
  source in the semantics of \citet{PadovaniN15}
  (\rocq{pn_deadlock_free_src}). A $\asTmId{fix}$ runs its body at every
  source unfolding, and the target forces its box at the same moment
  (\rocq{PNSimulation.tr_fix}), so the relation is closed under unfolding for every
  body.
\end{proof}

Their $\asTmId{fibo}$ and their signal pipe are mechanized
(\rocq{fibo_deadlock_free_src}, \rocq{pipe_deadlock_free_src}), and so
are three fixes whose body is no $\lambda$
(\rocq{div_deadlock_free_src}, \rocq{divsend_deadlock_free_src},
\rocq{fixfork_deadlock_free_src}).

\Citet{PadovaniN15} measure the same two bounds with numbers. A channel
type $p[t]^n$ carries a level $n$, the arrow $t \to^{\rho,\sigma} s$
carries the least level of a channel in the closure and the greatest
level the call acts on, and their $\asTmId{new}$, $\asTmId{send}$ and
$\asTmId{recv}$ each ask $n < \lvert t \rvert$, with
$\lvert t \rvert$ the level of $t$. Our $\lbd$ and $\ubd$ carry the
arrow's two bounds as points, and the premise $\tSend$ owes,
$\fLt{\pntA}{\pnode}$ for every head $\pnode$ of the payload, is
$n < \lvert t \rvert$ with the arithmetic removed. An abstract order
subsumes that condition rather than meeting it. Level polymorphism in
their system is the shift $\mathord{\Uparrow}^n$, admitted at a function
of level $\top$ only, and every shift is a fresh number. Here one
constraint $\cstr$ over the point variables of a $\Box$ states the facts
its instances owe, and a $\tForce$ at any instance that satisfies
$\cstr$ is legal, so the one constraint the translation writes covers
every shift the source takes, the unboundedly many of a recursive
definition included (\rocq{PNWorld.Crel}, \rocq{PNWorld.crel_hold}). A $\Box$ binds the
$\forall$ and holds no channel, which is the part their level $\top$
plays.

The context-free system of \citet{MordidoP} assigns priorities to the
actions of a context-free session type. We relate it to \OurLang\
informally. A priority becomes a point, the sequence of priorities their
$\asTmId{new}$ assigns to a channel becomes the offsets of its two end
points, which the translation identifies, and
their top effect becomes a ceiling (\cref{sec:ext-cfst}). Their examples
type here, the tree channel (\rocq{tree_deadlock_free}), the two streams
(\rocq{client_typed}, \rocq{newclient_typed}) and the repaired classic
deadlock (\rocq{dlfix_deadlock_free}).
